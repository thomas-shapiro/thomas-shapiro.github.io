Over the past ~8 years I have participated in various projects, created countless physical and digital models of geometric shapes, and carried out several formal mathematical research projects. I am currently studying Robotics & AI at AAU, Austria.

Projects:

  • Canonical Near-miss Johnson solids (2026).A census of convex polyhedra that almost have regular faces, measured in their canonical form. Hosted on Wagyx Xygaw's blog.
  • Catalan-like Polyhedra (2026).An interactive viewer for Catalan-like polyhedra produced by stellating fullerenes. Rotate, morph, compare, download.
  • Equal Earth (2026).An interactive viewer for a sphere divided into 195 segments of equal area. Each segment corresponds to a country on Earth. The project is purely art, not political.
  • NMJ-builder (2026).A tool that lets the user assemble canonical near-miss Johnson's solids with non-flat and non-hyperbolic vertices out of polygons - one by one or by symmetry group.
  • Alphabetical Dice (2023).Fair or near-fair specialty dice with all sorts of weird things on them. All models are available for free.
  • A visual index of all noble polyhedra (2023).I designed the big table that features all 146 non-exotic finite noble polyhedra as well as the infinite families. No noble polyhedron was discovered by me, although I was somewhat involved in the process: I provided images and mapped out the convex hull and duality relationships between all noble polyhedra. Connor Hill is the author of the completeness proof.
  • Near-miss uniform polytopes (2021).A non-exhaustive list of isogonal polytopes with near-equal edge lengths that do not become degenerate when the edge length ratio is minimized.
  • My old YouTube channel (2021).A small teenage project of mine.
